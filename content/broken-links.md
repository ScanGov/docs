ScanGov checks every link found while scanning your site and flags the ones that fail, so you can find and fix dead links before your visitors do.

## Filtering the list

Use the tabs at the top of the broken links page to narrow what you see: Confirmed, Likely, Needs review, Redirects, Dismissed, or All. Each tab's count reflects only what that tab shows.

Every link gets a status badge showing what ScanGov found and how confident it is. [Status](/status/#link-check-confidence) explains what each badge means.

## Rechecking a link

Click recheck on any link to re-verify it right away, instead of waiting for the next full site scan. ScanGov re-reads the page the link was found on and re-tests the link. If you fix or remove the link from the page, it drops off the list.

## Dismissing a link

Dismiss a flagged link if it's a false positive, an intentional choice, or a third-party site's problem. You can record why. Dismissed links move to the Dismissed tab and stop counting toward your broken link total. Undismiss a link at any time to bring it back.

## Redirects

A link that redirects to another address gets its own tab, alongside the address it redirects to, so you can review redirected links separately from links that fail outright.
